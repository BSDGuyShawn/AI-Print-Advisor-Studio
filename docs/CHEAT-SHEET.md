# Cheat sheet

One page. Print it or keep it open next to the app.

## The 6 steps to a good print

| # | Do this | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Open** your `.3mf` (drag it in, or **Open 3MF**) | top right |
| 2 | **Set T1–T4** to the filament you'll load | left: Toolheads and materials |
| 3 | Click **Overhangs**, then pick the **"lowest risk"** orientation | above the model · left: Orientation |
| 4 | Slide **Quality ↔ Speed**, say what it's for | left: What matters more? |
| 5 | **Get recommended settings**, untick what you don't want | left, then right: Recommendations |
| 6 | **Save as 3MF** or **Send to printer…** | right |

Optional: **Compare slices** (right) to see Fine / Fast / Recommended side by side first.

## Common problems → what to try

| Problem | Try |
|---|---|
| Lots of **red** in Overhangs | Pick another **orientation**; if still red, keep **supports** on. |
| Print **comes loose** / falls over | Choose an orientation with more **bed contact**; accept the **brim** the app suggests. |
| **Spaghetti** mess | Usually overhangs without supports, or a print that came loose. Use the **troubleshooter**. |
| **Stringing** (hairs between parts) | Dry the filament; the troubleshooter suggests retraction/temperature changes. |
| Takes **too long** | Slide toward **Speed**, or pick **Fast** in Compare slices. |
| Looks **rough** | Slide toward **Quality**, or pick **Fine**. |
| MakerWorld file shows **warnings in Orca** | Open it in this app first; it converts it for the U1. |
| "Snapmaker Orca wasn't found" | Settings → **Browse** to `snapmaker-orca.exe`. |
| AI button does nothing / asks for a key | Settings → paste your **API key**, or choose **Free chatbot**. |
| Windows says **"protected your PC"** | **More info → Run anyway**. Or before extracting: zip → right-click → **Properties** → **Unblock** (see FAQ). |

## Words

**Overhang**: printed over air · **Supports**: scaffolding you break off · **Brim**: sticky ring around the base · **Infill**: inside pattern (10–20%) · **Walls**: outer shell lines (2–3) · **Layer height**: 0.08 smooth … 0.28 fast. More in the [glossary](USER-GUIDE.md#glossary).

**Don't know a word?** Hover over any word with a dotted underline in the app, or click **Glossary** at the top.
