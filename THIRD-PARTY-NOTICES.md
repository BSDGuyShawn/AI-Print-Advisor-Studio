## Data derived from Snapmaker Orca

`src/orca-schema.json` (setting names, types, ranges and allowed values) is generated by `tools/extract-schema.py` from `src/libslic3r/PrintConfig.cpp` and `src/libslic3r/Preset.cpp` in [Snapmaker/OrcaSlicer](https://github.com/Snapmaker/OrcaSlicer), licensed AGPL-3.0. Snapmaker Orca is based on OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio and PrusaSlicer.

`src/u1-template-settings.json` is a settings file produced by Snapmaker Orca's own command line from its bundled Snapmaker U1 profiles.

The app does not include Snapmaker Orca itself. It uses the copy you install.
